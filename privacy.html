ICYMP — Privacy Policy

ICYMP · operated by ICYTECH Media Production · Last updated: 3 October 2026

ICYMP is a desktop application that creates karaoke videos on the user's own computer and, when the user asks it to, publishes them to the user's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what data ICYMP accesses and how it is handled.

1. YouTube API Services

ICYMP uses YouTube API Services. By using ICYMP's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

After you sign in with your Google account (OAuth 2.0) and grant permission, ICYMP accesses only your own channel:

  • Channel information — channel ID, name, URL and verification status, to show which channel is connected.
  • Your uploaded videos — video IDs, titles, descriptions and publish dates, to see which songs are already on the channel and avoid duplicate uploads.
  • Uploading and editing — uploading the videos you choose, setting their thumbnails, adding the song lyrics to their descriptions, creating artist playlists and adding your videos to them.
  • Comments — writing the channel's first comment under your own videos and checking whether it already exists.

ICYMP does not access other users' data, your subscribers, your watch history, YouTube Analytics or any non-public information other than your own channel's.

2. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic account information (such as display name and avatar) — only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Permission to upload / post videos — only for the videos you choose, with the caption and visibility you select.

3. Where data is stored

ICYMP has no servers and collects nothing for ICYTECH Media Production. Everything stays on your own computer: the access tokens issued by Google and TikTok after you sign in, and a local cache of your own channel's video list (IDs, titles, descriptions, dates). The cache is refreshed whenever you scan the channel and is never kept unrefreshed for more than 30 days. ICYMP does not use cookies, and it does not place, access or recognize cookies or similar technologies on your device; it only stores the local files described here in its own program folder.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, internally or externally. Data is sent only to YouTube (Google) and TikTok through their official APIs, and only when you start an action.

5. Your control, revocation and deletion

  • Google / YouTube: you can revoke ICYMP's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • "Sign out" in ICYMP deletes the locally stored token immediately. All other local data can be deleted by deleting ICYMP's data folder. If you revoke access, ICYMP stops using the data and you can delete the local files at any time; on request we will help you delete them within 7 days.

6. Children

ICYMP is not intended for users under 18.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
