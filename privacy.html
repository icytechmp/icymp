ICYMP — Privacy Policy

ICYMP · operated by ICYTECH Media Production · Last updated: 2 October 2026

ICYMP is a desktop application that creates karaoke videos on the user's own computer and, when the user asks it to, sends those videos to the user's own TikTok account as drafts. This policy explains what data ICYMP accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic account information (such as your display name and avatar) — only to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos — only to send the videos you choose to your TikTok account as drafts. You review and publish them yourself in the TikTok app.

ICYMP does not read your messages, followers, contacts or any other TikTok data.

2. Where data is stored

ICYMP has no servers. The access token TikTok issues after you log in is stored only in a local file on your own computer. Videos are created and stored only on your computer until you send them.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, and only when you start an upload.

4. Your control

  • You can disconnect ICYMP at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • You can delete the locally stored token by using "Log out" in ICYMP or by deleting its data folder.

5. Children

ICYMP is not intended for users under 18.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
