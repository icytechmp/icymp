ICYMP

ICYMP · operated by ICYTECH Media Production · Last updated: 3 October 2026

ICYMP is a desktop tool by ICYTECH Media Production for the owner of the ICYTECH karaoke channels. It creates karaoke / instrumental videos on the owner's own computer and publishes them to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube — uploads the videos and Shorts to the owner's YouTube channel, sets the thumbnail, writes the channel's first comment, adds each video to the artist's playlist and adds the song lyrics to the video description. ICYMP uses YouTube API Services.
  • TikTok — sends the videos to the owner's TikTok account (draft or direct post).

By using ICYMP's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
