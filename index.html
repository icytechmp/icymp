About ICYMP

ICYMP is a Windows desktop application developed by ICYTECH Media Production. It is the production tool behind the ICYTECH karaoke channels: it turns a song into a finished karaoke / instrumental video on the creator's own computer and then publishes that video to the creator's own accounts. Everything runs locally — ICYMP has no servers and does not collect data.

Features

🎤 Karaoke production

Separates vocals from the music, optionally changes the key, and prepares a clean instrumental track.

📝 Synced lyrics

Lines up the song lyrics with the music so every word lights up on screen at the right moment.

🎬 Video rendering

Renders full-length karaoke videos and short vertical clips with title cards, credits and thumbnails.

▶️ YouTube publishing

Uploads to the creator's own channel, sets the thumbnail, adds the lyrics to the description and keeps artist playlists in order.

🎵 TikTok publishing

Posts the videos to the creator's own TikTok account directly, or sends them to the TikTok inbox as drafts.

🔒 Local and private

Files, settings and sign-in tokens stay on the creator's computer. Signing out deletes the stored token.

How it works

  1. Pick a song. Add the songs you want to produce to the ICYMP list.
  2. Produce. ICYMP prepares the instrumental, syncs the lyrics and renders the video on your PC.
  3. Review. Watch the result, edit the title, caption and lyrics if needed.
  4. Publish. Choose YouTube and/or TikTok, confirm the settings, and ICYMP uploads the video to your own account.

TikTok integration

ICYMP uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API:

  • Sign in with TikTok — your system browser opens TikTok's own consent page; ICYMP then shows the connected account's display name and avatar so you know where videos will go.
  • You stay in control — before posting, ICYMP shows the privacy options returned by TikTok (no default is pre-selected), the comment / duet / stitch settings, the commercial content disclosure and an editable caption. Nothing is posted until you confirm.
  • Direct post or draft — post straight to your profile, or send the video to your TikTok inbox to finish and post it in the TikTok app.
  • Sign out at any time — “Sign out” in ICYMP deletes the local token; you can also remove access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

YouTube integration

ICYMP uses YouTube API Services. By using ICYMP's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Which platforms does ICYMP run on?

ICYMP is a desktop application for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Does ICYMP store my data on a server?

No. ICYMP has no servers. Your videos, settings and sign-in tokens stay on your own computer. See the Privacy Policy.

Can ICYMP post to someone else's account?

No. You can only connect accounts and channels that you own or are authorized to manage, and every upload is started by you.

How do I disconnect TikTok or YouTube?

Click “Sign out” in ICYMP, or revoke access from your TikTok or Google account settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is ICYMP affiliated with TikTok or YouTube?

No. ICYMP uses their official APIs but is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by TikTok, YouTube or Google.
